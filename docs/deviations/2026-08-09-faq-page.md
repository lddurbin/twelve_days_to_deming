## 2026-08-09 — Add an FAQ page with FAQPage structured data

- **What** — A new root page, `faq.qmd`, answers twelve common reader questions (what the course is, who Deming and Neave were, cost, time commitment, statistics prerequisites, study groups, the Red Bead and funnel experiments, the 14 Points, the System of Profound Knowledge, and the relationship to the printed Workbook) as one H2 per question with a self-contained 2-4 sentence answer. It's paired with `FAQPage` JSON-LD (`assets/schema/faq-jsonld.html`, wired in via `include-in-header`) so the question/answer structure is machine-readable to answer engines, not just visually scannable.
- **Where** — New file `faq.qmd` at the project root, added to `book.chapters` in `_quarto-en.yml` immediately after `changes-from-source.qmd`, and enumerated in `.pa11yci.json`. New file `assets/schema/faq-jsonld.html` holds the JSON-LD, following the same `include-in-header` mechanism already used by `index.qmd` and `assets/schema/course-jsonld.html`. The page links into the course proper wherever an existing `{#sec-pageN}` anchor covers the topic (Day 2's Red Bead walkthrough, Day 3's funnel experiment, Days 4-5's 14 Points, Day 9's System of Profound Knowledge), and cross-links the existing [glossary](/content/appendix/glossary.qmd) entry for System of Profound Knowledge. The reciprocal cross-link to the concepts index page (a separate page tracked in [#509](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/509)) is deliberately **not** added here — that page didn't exist on `main` at the time this PR was built, since it was being developed concurrently in a sibling branch. It's deferred to a small fast-follow PR once #509 lands, following the precedent set by [#510](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/510)'s implementation PR #574 and its tiny follow-up PR #575.
- **Source reference** — n/a. This content never existed in Neave's PDFs; the questions and answers originate from the course's existing site content (`welcome.qmd`, `index.qmd`, `about-this-edition.qmd`, `what-this-course-covers.qmd`, and the relevant day chapters), rewritten and condensed for direct extraction rather than transcribed.
- **Why** — Part of the SEO/GEO discoverability epic [#497](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/497): answer engines cite self-contained, question-shaped statements, but the site's 658 headings are only ~5% question-shaped, and most of those are `PAUSE FOR THOUGHT` / `ACTIVITY` headings that pose a question to the reader with no answer on the page — the exact inverse of what an answer engine extracts. Neave's prose can't and shouldn't be reshaped to fix this, but the site already has an established category of Lee-authored editorial pages (`about-this-edition.qmd`, `accessibility.qmd`, `privacy.qmd`, `changes-from-source.qmd`, `what-this-course-covers.qmd`) that carry no transcription constraint, and an FAQ page is a natural extension of that category.
