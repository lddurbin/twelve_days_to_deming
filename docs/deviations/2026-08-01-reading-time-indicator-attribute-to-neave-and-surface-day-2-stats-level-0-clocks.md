## 2026-08-01 — Reword the reading-time indicator to name Neave; surface Day 2's second clock column only where it changes the number

- **What** — Two changes to the chapter-top reading-time indicator. (1) The second figure is no longer labelled "recommended": `~ 7 min reading · ~ 35 min recommended` becomes `~ 7 min reading · ~ 35 min at Neave's pace`, with the `aria-label`/`title` tooltip expanded from *"Estimated reading time and the author's recommended time…"* to *"About 7 minutes to read the text. Dr Neave's course allows about 35 minutes for this section, including reflection and activities."* (2) Added an optional `session_minutes_stats0:` front-matter field, rendering as a parenthetical — `~ 12 min reading · ~ 45 min at Neave's pace (~ 30 min on Stats-level 0)` — set on exactly two Day 2 chapters. The single-number collapse form (values within ±1 min) and the no-clock-data fallback (`~ N min reading [+ activities]`) are unchanged, so Days 3–12 render byte-identically to before.
- **Where** — `filters/reading-time.lua` (`indicator_block()`; `read_session_minutes()` generalised to `read_minutes_field(meta, key)`); `content/days/day-02/05-the-truth-the-whole-truth-and-nothing-but.qmd` (`session_minutes_stats0: 30`) and `content/days/day-02/07-some-recollections.qmd` (`session_minutes_stats0: 90`); `about-this-edition.qmd` ("Reading-time indicator" section rewritten); `assets/styles/main.css` (`text-wrap: pretty` on `.reading-time`, since the longest form now runs ~70 characters); `workflow/PATTERNS.md` and `assets/templates/chapter-template.qmd` (docs); `workflow/briefs/day-02-brief.yml` (Stats-level 0 computation table added alongside the existing Stats-level 1–3 one); `workflow/inter-day-refs.csv` (regenerated — front-matter additions shifted line numbers in the Day 2 chapters).
- **Source reference** — Day 2's pages carry **two** columns of clock faces, not one: the left column paces readers who self-classified as Stats-level 0 in Neave's [Welcome](/welcome.qmd) (point 9), the right column paces Stats-levels 1–3. Both columns were dictated in full from the original book and are recorded under `clock_reads:` in `workflow/briefs/day-02-brief.yml` — 26 checkpoints for Stats-level 0 (9:15am–4:45pm) and 27 for Stats-level 1–3 (9:00am–5:00pm). Running the `session_minutes` computation over each track separately shows the two agree on 6 of the 8 settable chapters; the entire 30-minute day-length difference sits in `05-the-truth-the-whole-truth-and-nothing-but` (45 vs 30 min — Stats-level 0 is told to skip the three Technical Aids, page 21: *"please now move straight on to page 22"*) and `07-some-recollections` (105 vs 90 min — Stats-level 0 looks the control limits up on Appendix page 8 rather than computing them).
- **Why** — On the wording: a participant in the live cohort asked what the second number meant. "Recommended" names neither who recommends nor why the two figures differ, and the explanation only existed on the About page. Naming Neave puts it in the indicator itself. On Day 2: Neave printed two full pacing tracks and we are surfacing the second one **only on the two chapters where it changes the number** — that partial representation is the deviation being logged. Rendering both figures on all nine chapters would put a second number on seven pages where it is identical to the first, which is noise rather than fidelity; rendering neither would silently drop one of Neave's two published tracks, which is what the site did until now. The fixed label "Stats-level 0" is used rather than a per-chapter explanation because the two chapters diverge for genuinely different reasons — one skips content, one does not — so any specific phrasing ("if you skip the Technical Aids") would be false on the other. Supersedes the wording described in [the 2026-04-28 `session_minutes` entry](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/blob/main/docs/deviations/2026-04-28-encode-session-minutes-per-chapter-from-neave-s-clock-budget.md); that entry's set/unset reasoning still stands.
