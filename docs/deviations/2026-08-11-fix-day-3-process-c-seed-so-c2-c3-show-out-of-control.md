## 2026-08-11 — Fix Day 3 Process C seed so Charts C2/C3 actually show out-of-control

- **What** — Changed the random seed for Process C (Red Beads) in `R/data/day-03-six-processes.R` from `set.seed(88)` to `set.seed(1170)`. No other Process C code changed: still 24 paddles for C1, then 18 normal paddles followed by 6 "doubled inspector count" paddles for C2.
- **Where** — `R/data/day-03-six-processes.R` (seed only, plus an expanded comment recording the sweep). No `.qmd` changes were needed — the prose in `content/days/day-03/09-the-six-processes-revisited.qmd` already stated the exact numeric claim ("the first actual count of red beads when I started doing this happened to be only 4, so Point 43 is at 8") that the old seed contradicted and the new seed now satisfies.
- **Source reference** — Neave Day 3 pages 19 and 30 (Charts C2 and C3). The prose makes specific, checkable claims about this chart: Chart C2 is one of six charts on page 19 meant to visibly demonstrate an out-of-control process; Chart C3's caption and body text state "the final five points jump well above the UCL" and that Point 43 "hides" the special cause because its doubled value (8, from a raw count of 4) still falls under the UCL.
- **Why** — A course participant (Viraj) reported that Charts C2 and C3 didn't seem to match the surrounding reading material. Reproducing `R/data/day-03-six-processes.R` under the old seed (88) confirmed the report: the UCL computed from Chart C1 was 20.85, and the six doubled points (43–48) were `16, 20, 16, 18, 14, 20` — **none** breached it. Chart C2's own self-computed UCL (20.53) was also never breached (max value 20). Worse, the qmd's literal "Point 43 is at 8" claim didn't hold either — under seed 88, Point 43's raw count was 8 (doubled: 16), not 4/8. The deviations-log entry that introduced seed 88 (`2026-05-26-replace-day-3-six-processes-6-2-panel-png-with-an-r-generate.md`) asserted the seed was "chosen so each panel reproduces the visual pattern Neave's prose identifies," but — unlike Process A's seed (274), which has a documented sweep against explicit numeric targets — that claim for Process C was never actually verified against the rendered numbers.
- **Fix method** — Swept seeds 1–20000, replaying the exact `set.seed(s); process_C1 <- draw_red_beads(24); process_C2 <- c(draw_red_beads(18), 2L * draw_red_beads(6))` sequence for each, filtering for: Point 43's doubled value stays under the C1-derived UCL (hides, per the prose); Points 44–48 all clearly exceed the C1-derived UCL with a margin (reads as "well above" rather than a marginal breach); all values stay inside the chart's `y_limits` of 0–30. Seed `1170` was the first candidate that also happened to match the prose's literal numbers exactly (raw count 4 → doubled 8 at Point 43) and additionally breaches Chart C2's own self-computed UCL at Points 44–48 (21.59) — a bonus not required by any specific textual claim, but it means the page-19 panel now shows a visible signal too, not just the page-30 extended chart.
- **Verification** — Rendered the C1/C2 panel and the C3 individual chart directly via `ggsave()` (bypassing a full `quarto render`) and visually confirmed: C1 is flat/stable throughout; C2 stays flat for 18 points then jumps clearly above its own UCL for the final 6; C3 shows the familiar pattern through Point 42, a dip at Point 43 (value 8, just below the centre line), then Points 44–48 well above the UCL — matching the prose word-for-word.
