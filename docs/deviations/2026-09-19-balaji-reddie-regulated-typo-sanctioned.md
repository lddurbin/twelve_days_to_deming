## 2026-09-19 — Balaji Reddie appendix "needed to regulated" typo sanctioned retroactively

- **What** — The site reads "the low-density lipoprotein needed to **be** *regulated*, not *destroyed*". The source drops a word and prints "needed to *regulated*". At some undetermined point the site silently added "be", and the change was never logged. The correction is kept, with no `[sic]` marker.
- **Where** — `content/appendix/contributions-balaji-reddie/01-prelude-a-system.qmd`, Prelude A.
- **Source reference** — `Q.Contributions.from.Balaji.Reddie.11Sep21.pdf`, printed page 6 (PDF page 10).
- **Why** — The intended reading is unambiguous. The text is Balaji Reddie's contribution, not Neave's prose, so reproducing the slip would put a transcription error in a named contributor's mouth. Under the rule settled in [#831](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/831), every typo correction gets an entry even when it is obvious. It is logged on the same basis as the [four Day 3 typos](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/blob/main/docs/deviations/2026-09-19-day-03-four-typos-sanctioned.md).
- **Decided in** — [#831](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/831) (2026-09-19), which settles Wave 2 Balaji Reddie finding `S-02` from [#833](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/833). That finding was waiting on this policy.
- **Landed in** — PR [#838](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/pull/838).
