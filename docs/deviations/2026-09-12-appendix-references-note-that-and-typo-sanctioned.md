## 2026-09-12 — References appendix "(Note that and 0 and 1…)" typo sanctioned retroactively

- **What** — `content/appendix/11-references-and-sources.qmd:132` reads "(Note that [0]{.underline} and [1]{.underline} are digits and [O]{.underline} and [I]{.underline} are capital letters.)" Neave's own PDF prints "(Note that **and** 0 and 1 are digits and O and I are capital letters.)" — a stray "and", almost certainly a half-finished edit. The site had already silently dropped it at some undetermined point, with no deviations-log entry recording it. Decision: keep the corrected reading rather than reproducing the literal source slip. No `[sic]` marker was added, since the site is not reproducing the original error verbatim.
- **Where** — `content/appendix/11-references-and-sources.qmd:132`.
- **Source reference** — `R.References.and.Sources.09Feb22.pdf`, printed page 4 (PDF page 8).
- **Why** — Epic #734's Wave 2 pass over the References and Sources appendix ([#804](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/804)) surfaced this as finding R-02, and it was first adjudicated the other way: restore the stray "and" and reproduce the page exactly. That was reversed on review. The sentence is Neave's own warning that "0 and 1 are digits and O and I are capital letters" — a note about reading his glyphs correctly — and a stray conjunction inside it makes the one sentence on the page whose whole job is precision read as though the site had fumbled it. Reproducing it verbatim buys fidelity at the cost of the sentence's function. This also keeps the appendix consistent with Day 7's "religious"/"religiously", Day 8's "echos"/"innovaton", Day 9's "Zeebruge"/"Zeebrugge" and Day 11's "syppose"/"Mazlow's" precedents, all of which kept the correction and logged it retroactively rather than reverting it.
- **Decided in** — Wave 2 References appendix adjudication pass, [#804](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/804) (2026-09-12): Lee initially accepted R-02 as "restore Neave's wording", then reversed it on review — "keep the correction, log it retroactively as a sanctioned typo".
