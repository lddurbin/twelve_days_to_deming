## 2026-09-12 — Restore Neave's guidance on the timing indications, adapted to the web edition

- **What** — Restored two of the three sentences that [`2026-04-28-remove-decorative-clock-icons-and-the-welcome-page-paragraph.md`](2026-04-28-remove-decorative-clock-icons-and-the-welcome-page-paragraph.md) removed from `index.qmd`, and replaced the third with a web-native equivalent written in Neave's own first person. The paragraph on printed Welcome page 4 now reads "… a red, white and blue box that you will not be able to miss! **Each chapter opens with an indication of the time I have allowed for it. Again, treat these indications as rough guides rather than instructions. I believe they will help you to pace yourself appropriately through the reading and particularly when carrying out the activities.** As indicated above, Day 1 is a little different …" The source's first sentence — "Little clock icons such as those I'm showing you against this paragraph will appear both on the opening page for each day …" — **stays removed**, since the icons it points at do not exist in this edition.
- **Where** — `index.qmd:132` ("Time management" → "Detailed guidance").
- **Source reference** — `A.PLEASE.START.HERE.08Feb22.pdf`, PDF page 12 (printed Welcome page 4). Confirmed on the page image, which shows the four clock faces in the margins beside this paragraph.
- **Why** — Epic [#734](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/734)'s Wave 2 pass over `index.qmd` ([#811](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/811), finding `I-08`) found that the 2026-04-28 excision **over-removed against its own stated rationale**. Only the first of the three sentences depends on the clock icons; the other two describe how to treat the *timings*. That entry's own reasoning was that "the chapter-top reading-time indicator (`filters/reading-time.lua`) already conveys actionable pacing" — and it does, rendering `~ N min reading · ~ M min at Neave's pace`, where `M` is the `session_minutes` front-matter field carried on 44 chapters and **derived from the very clocks that were removed**. So the mechanism survived the removal and only Neave's guidance on how to read it was lost, leaving the site's timing indications explained nowhere at all. Restoring the guidance closes that gap without reintroducing the icons.
- **Decided in** — Wave 2 `index` adjudication pass, [#811](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/issues/811) (2026-09-12): Lee accepted `I-08`, with the note that the substituted sentence "should be written in the first [person] (i.e. as if from Neave), or else formatted as an editorial note" — hence "the time **I** have allowed for it" rather than the third-person phrasing originally proposed.
