## 2026-09-22 — Three Day 3 emphasis differences checked and kept

- **What** — Day 3's emphasis pass restored 188 of Neave's emphasis runs. Three differences were read against the page images and deliberately left as the site has them:
  1. **"Variation is indeed the enemy of quality."** The site sets the whole closing line bold-italic. The source sets it bold-italic *except* for the word "of", which is bold upright — almost certainly a slip in the typesetting rather than a distinction Neave intended. Reproducing it would print `***Variation is indeed the enemy*** **of** ***quality***`.
  2. **"all your Rule 4 outcomes"** in the Rules 3 and 4 Major Activity. The source sets "outcome" in bold red, where every other occurrence of the word in that activity is plain red — so the bold is carrying a distinction the colour is not. The site reproduces none of Neave's funnel/marble/outcome colour-coding, so the bold would arrive without the red around it, and the word stays plain until that question is settled.
  3. **Deming's heading "Faulty inspection caused by fear"** is bold blue and upright in the source; the site sets it bold italic. This is the site-wide convention of italicising Deming's blue text, not a Day 3 decision — it is recorded here because it is the third of the three emphasis differences a reader comparing Day 3 against the PDF will find.
