## 2026-09-12 — A live PDF host was reported as dead, and its acronym explained by an invented organisation

- **What** — Neave's Welcome booklet (printed p6) names four sites hosting the *12 Days to Deming* PDFs, the last being `www.nzoq.org.nz` — the New Zealand Organisation for Quality, the expansion he gives in the same parenthesis. This edition had transcribed that address as `www.rqoq.org.nz`. Three consequences followed, all now corrected:
  1. `welcome.qmd` carried the wrong address, de-linked, with an editorial note reading "this domain is no longer online and no archived snapshot is available". The address is restored, linked, and the note removed.
  2. The public [Changes from the source](https://deming.leedurbin.co.nz/changes-from-source.html) page listed `rqoq.org.nz` among the dead external links and glossed it as "the Royal Quality Organisation of Queensland" — **an organisation that does not exist**, invented to rationalise the unfamiliar acronym. That bullet is withdrawn and replaced with an account of what actually happened.
  3. `2026-04-23-dead-external-links-to-deming-org-uk-and-rqoq-org-nz.md` recorded the de-linking as a deliberate editorial decision. It is renamed and amended; its `deming.org.uk` half is unaffected and stands.
- **Where** — `welcome.qmd`, `changes-from-source.qmd`, `docs/deviations/2026-04-23-dead-external-links-to-deming-org-uk.md`.
- **Source reference** — `WELCOME-BOOKLET-(Aug21b).pdf`, printed p6, "12 Days to Deming 'HOSTS'": "All of the *12 Days to Deming* PDF files as listed above may be viewed at, and downloaded from, any of the websites www.spcpress.com, www.theuic.com/deming, www.sergentresults.com/12-days-to-deming and, most recently, www.nzoq.org.nz (the New Zealand Organisation for Quality)."
- **Why** — This is logged as a material departure rather than a silent typo fix because the mistranscription did not stay a typo. A dead-link audit (#239, PR #242) checked the transcribed domain, found it unresolvable with zero Wayback captures — because it had never existed — and reasonably concluded the mirror had died. For five months the site therefore told readers that one of Neave's four official hosts was gone, and the deviations log documented that as an intentional decision. `https://www.nzoq.org.nz` returns HTTP 200 and `https://www.nzoq.org.nz/12-days-to-deming` still serves the PDFs; the repo's own `README.md` and `about-this-edition.qmd` had the correct URL throughout, describing NZOQ as "the upstream source of record", so the site contradicted itself. Recording the withdrawal matters as much as the fix: a reader who saw the old page was told something false about where to obtain Neave's material.
- **Decided in** — #809 adjudication findings `W-01`, `W-18` and `W-32` (2026-09-12), all accepted.
- **Landed in** — PR [#810](https://github.com/lddurbin/twelve_days_to_deming/pull/810).
